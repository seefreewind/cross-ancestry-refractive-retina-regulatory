# Retinal OCR claim boundary

Final verdict: PASS WITH BOUNDED WORDING

## Evidence

The all-retinal-OCR S-LDXR estimate from the v1.0 analysis was:

| Context | GCORSQ | SE | 95% CI |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Genome-wide | 1.009945 | 0.108276 | 0.797725–1.222165 |
| All retinal OCR | 0.935405 | 0.101099 | 0.737250–1.133559 |

No formal difference test between genome-wide and all-retinal-OCR GCORSQ was identified in the current outputs.

## Claim boundary

Supported:

> Broad retinal OCRs showed an S-LDXR estimate broadly compatible with the genome-wide pattern of shared architecture.

Not supported:

> Broad retinal OCRs have statistically equivalent architecture to the genome-wide background.

Not supported:

> There is no difference between retinal OCR and genome-wide architecture.
