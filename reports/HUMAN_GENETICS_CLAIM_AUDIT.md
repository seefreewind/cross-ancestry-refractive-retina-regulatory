# Human Genetics claim audit

Status: READY WITH CONTROLLED WORDING

## Claim A — Strong genome-wide sharing

Supported. EUR LDSC h2 = 0.1219 (SE = 0.0072, Z = 16.93), EAS LDSC h2 = 0.1494 (SE = 0.0119, Z = 12.55), and genome-wide S-LDXR GCORSQ = 1.009945 (SE = 0.108276) support predominantly shared EUR-EAS common-variant architecture.

Required wording: “boundary-adjacent estimate compatible with very high sharing under an unbounded estimator.”

Avoid wording that treats an estimator above 1 as a literal biological value.

## Claim B — Broad retinal OCR compatible with shared architecture

Supported. All-retinal-OCR S-LDXR GCORSQ = 0.935405 (SE = 0.101099), compatible with the genome-wide shared-architecture pattern.

Required wording: “broadly compatible with the genome-wide pattern of shared architecture.”

Avoid wording that claims perfect identity across ancestries.

## Claim C — DAR excess heterogeneity not detected

Supported. Primary ancestry-DAR analysis: 71/1,232 DAR SNPs and 19,653/379,279 matched non-DAR retinal OCR SNPs in the top 5% heterogeneity endpoint; OR = 1.119, 95% CI = 0.867–1.423, Fisher P = 0.367; matched permutation empirical P = 0.334; continuous chi2_het P = 0.711.

Required wording: “did not show detectable excess EUR-EAS effect heterogeneity under the frozen matched analysis.”

Avoid wording that claims complete absence of heterogeneity.

## Claim D — Large OR ≥ 1.5 DAR enrichment excluded

Supported for the frozen top 5% endpoint. The observed 95% CI upper bound was 1.423, below the prespecified large-enrichment boundary OR = 1.5.

Required wording: “large enrichment of OR ≥ 1.5 is inconsistent with the observed confidence interval.”

## Claim E — Modest enrichment remains possible

Supported and required. Minimum detectable OR was 1.397 for 80% power and 1.467 for 90% power. The design cannot exclude smaller effects.

Required wording: “modest enrichment remains possible.”

## Claim F — Ancestry-associated regulatory variation does not necessarily imply ancestry-divergent trait effects

Supported as an interpretation, not a universal causal conclusion. The frozen analysis supports this statement for refractive error, EUR-EAS comparison and the HRCA-derived retinal DAR annotation.

Required wording: “need not translate into a large systematic concentration of ancestry-divergent GWAS effects.”

Avoid wording that turns this project-specific interpretation into a universal causal rule.
